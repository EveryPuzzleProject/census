A daily that took up the crossword in November 1924. The Newspaper Navigator dataset ([research-notes-newspaper-navigator.md](../research-notes-newspaper-navigator.md))
finds a puzzle on 477 dates: 245 of them in 1925, almost none from 1927 to 1931, then 16 to
74 a year from 1932 to 1937. Whether the gap is the paper's or the dataset's is not known;
for the Washington Evening Star a gap like it turned out to be the dataset's.

The 1925 heading and layout match the New Britain Herald's of the same year
([new-britain-herald.md](new-britain-herald.md)), and the 1930s puzzles, each built around a
named person or subject with a picture in the grid, match those in the Indianapolis Times
and the Hendersonville Times-News. That points to one seller for each period; no syndicate
or copyright line was seen on the crops, so which one is not established.

Open questions: the syndicate; whether the puzzle ran in 1927–31; when it ended.
