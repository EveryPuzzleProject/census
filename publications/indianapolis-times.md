The Times introduced its crossword on October 14, 1924 and promised one every day from the
next. The Newspaper Navigator dataset ([research-notes-newspaper-navigator.md](../research-notes-newspaper-navigator.md)) finds a puzzle on 459 dates: 192 in 1925, none
in 1927–28, then 46 in 1930 and 94 in 1931, thinning to 1935. The gaps have not been checked
against the pages.

The 1935 puzzle is the same kind as the Bismarck Tribune's and the Hendersonville
Times-News's of those years: a person or subject as the theme, with a picture in the grid.
No syndicate or copyright line was seen on the crops.

Open questions: the syndicate in each period; whether the daily puzzle ran without a break;
when it ended.
