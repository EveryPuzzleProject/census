Two different bought-in puzzles. In January 1940 the Star ran the themed puzzle with a
picture in the grid that the Hendersonville Times-News and others carried through the
1930s. By September 1942 it ran a 13-square "Daily Crossword" with a Cryptoquote under it,
credited "Distributed by King Features Syndicate, Inc."
([King Features](../syndicates/king-features.md)). When it changed over was not looked for.
The Newspaper Navigator dataset ([research-notes-newspaper-navigator.md](../research-notes-newspaper-navigator.md)) finds a puzzle on only 102 dates, so most of the run
is still to be located on the pages.

Open questions: the first syndicate; the date of the change; the first and last dates.
