A weekly that ran the Western Newspaper Union crossword from December 1924 to at least
April 1926: the same ready-print series as the Newport Mercury's
([newport-mercury.md](newport-mercury.md); see
[research-notes-weekly-syndication.md](../research-notes-weekly-syndication.md)), starting
three weeks earlier here. Found through the Newspaper Navigator dataset ([research-notes-newspaper-navigator.md](../research-notes-newspaper-navigator.md)), which has
no detection after 1926-04-30 though it holds 1927's issues.

Open questions: whether the puzzles match the Mercury's week for week; the last date.
