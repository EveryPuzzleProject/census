A small-city daily with a themed crossword through the 1930s: each puzzle is built around a
person or subject, often with a picture in the grid, and carries the answer to the one
before. The same kind of puzzle is in the Bismarck Tribune and the Indianapolis Times in
those years and in the Wilmington Morning Star in 1940, so it was bought from a syndicate;
no syndicate line was seen on the crops. The Newspaper Navigator dataset ([research-notes-newspaper-navigator.md](../research-notes-newspaper-navigator.md)) finds it on
117 dates, a fraction of the run if it was daily.

Open questions: the syndicate; the first and last dates (the scans in the dataset run
1933–1938, and the puzzle is there at both ends).
