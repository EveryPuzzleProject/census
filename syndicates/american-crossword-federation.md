Stan Newman's own outfit, listing the Newsday crossword he edited from 1990, a year
before Creators Syndicate lists it too (see [creators.md](creators.md)). One of the
editors in the 1980s–2000s who sold their own puzzle, with Merl Reagle and later Wayne
Robert Williams.

It also published Newman's subscription newsletter, Crossworder's OWN Newsletter, from
at least January 1984; renamed Tough Puzzles in 1991, it ran to 1994 or later. Dan
Feyer transcribed 85 of its puzzles and posted them with Newman's permission in 2020.

Open questions: when the newsletter began (Vol. I, No. 1 is presumably late 1983) and
ended; whether the ACF ran Newman's tournaments too; how long the ACF lasted.
