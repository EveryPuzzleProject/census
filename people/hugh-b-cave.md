**Evidence.** The byline gives an uncommon full name, an age and a town: 13 years old,
Brookline. Cave was born on 11 July 1910, so he was 13 on 13 April 1924, three months
short of 14. His family moved to the Boston area during the First World War, and he
attended Brookline High School (Wikipedia). The puzzle is the Globe's Sunday crossword,
three days after Simon and Schuster's first crossword book came out. Its page carries a
note that the Globe also ran puzzles on Wednesdays and Saturdays. Found while indexing
the Globe for the New York World run (newyorkworld `printings.tsv`).

Nothing yet says in Cave's own words that he made crosswords. His Wikipedia article
doesn't mention them, and says sources differ on when he first sold a story. That makes
this byline possibly his earliest known publication, but that isn't established.
